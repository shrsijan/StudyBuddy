# App Idea Brainstorming

## Initial Brainstorming Ideas

1. **StudyBuddy** - A gamified study group app for college students. Users find and create study sessions, check in when they arrive, earn XP and streak bonuses for consistent attendance, and climb course-specific leaderboards. Rewards and friendly competition keep students motivated and accountable.

2. **MealSplit** - A bill-splitting app for group dining. Users snap a photo of the receipt, the app parses items, and each person selects what they ordered to split costs fairly.

3. **ParkSpot** - A community-driven parking finder. Users report open parking spots in real-time so nearby drivers can snag them quickly.

4. **FitCircle** - A social fitness app where friends create workout challenges, track progress together, and cheer each other on with reactions.

5. **EventDrop** - A hyperlocal event discovery app. Users pin spontaneous events (pickup basketball, live music, food trucks) on a map for others nearby to find.

6. **SkillSwap** - A peer-to-peer skill exchange platform. Students offer skills they have (e.g., guitar lessons) in exchange for skills they want (e.g., coding help).

---

## Evaluation of Top 3 Ideas

### 1. StudyBuddy
- **Mobile:** Location-based check-ins verify attendance at study sessions, push notifications for streak reminders and leaderboard updates, real-time chat. Strongly mobile-first.
- **Story:** College students struggle to stay motivated studying alone. StudyBuddy turns study sessions into a rewarding habit — check in, earn points, maintain streaks, and compete on leaderboards. It builds community *and* accountability through gamification.
- **Market:** Large and well-defined — college students. Every student takes courses and many want study partners. Gamification appeals especially to the competitive and achievement-driven segment.
- **Habit:** Very high frequency — the streak system and daily check-in rewards incentivize opening the app every day. Leaderboard competition drives repeat engagement beyond just exam season.
- **Scope:** Core MVP (sessions + check-ins + points) is well-scoped. Leaderboards and streak tracking add moderate complexity. Badges and rewards tiers are stretch goals that layer on top cleanly.

### 2. MealSplit
- **Mobile:** Camera integration for receipt scanning, instant notifications for payment requests. Good mobile use case.
- **Story:** Splitting bills is a universal pain point. Clear value proposition.
- **Market:** Broad — anyone who eats out with friends. But competes with Venmo/Splitwise.
- **Habit:** Occasional use, only when dining out in groups.
- **Scope:** Receipt OCR parsing is technically complex and could derail the timeline.

### 3. EventDrop
- **Mobile:** Map-based, location-driven, real-time — inherently mobile.
- **Story:** Helps people discover spontaneous things happening around them. Fun and social.
- **Market:** Young adults in urban/campus areas. Needs critical mass of users to be useful.
- **Habit:** Could be daily for curious users, but content depends on active posters.
- **Scope:** Map integration and real-time updates are feasible but need careful scoping.

---

## Final App Idea Chosen

**StudyBuddy**

We chose StudyBuddy because it has the strongest combination of a well-defined target market (college students), high usage frequency (daily/weekly during the semester), and a manageable technical scope. The gamification layer — check-ins, XP, streaks, and leaderboards — transforms it from a simple session finder into a habit-forming app that keeps students coming back. The core features are buildable within our timeline, while badges, reward tiers, and map views provide room to stretch.
